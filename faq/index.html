FAQ

Questions

How long does a project take?

Short-form videos: 2–4 days. Brand videos: 5–10 days. Color-only projects: 2–5 days depending on footage volume. Rush delivery is available.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds on every package. Additional rounds are billed separately.

Can you work with poorly shot footage?

Yes. Building a strong story from imperfect material is one of my strengths — through careful selects, pacing, sound and color.

How do I send footage?

Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, Frame.io — whatever works for you. I confirm everything has arrived before starting.

What formats do you deliver?

Any aspect ratio and platform: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5. Masters up to 4K, plus platform-ready versions.

Which software do you use?

Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and the Adobe suite. I can also work inside your project files and templates.

What time zone do you work in?

I work remotely with clients worldwide and adapt to your schedule. Communication via Telegram or the channel you prefer.

How does payment work?

50% upfront and 50% before final delivery. Monthly packages are paid at the start of the month.

Who owns the final video?

You do. Full rights transfer to you after final payment.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — happy to sign one before seeing your materials.
